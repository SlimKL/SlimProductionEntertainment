ABOUT US

The story and vision behind Slim Production Entertainment.

WHO WE ARE

Slim Production Entertainment is an independent record label and music production company focused on creating quality music, developing artists and building a strong entertainment brand.


Our services include music production, recording, mixing, mastering, artist development and music release support.

OUR MISSION

To create professional music, support talented artists and build opportunities for independent creators.

OUR VISION

To build Slim Production Entertainment into a respected African music company with international reach.
